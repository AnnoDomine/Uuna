# 🧙‍♂️ The Sages

![Sages](../images/sages.svg)

[⬅️ Back to Home](../Home.md)

The Sages act as the supreme authority on knowledge quality. They are the final gatekeepers before information is presented to the user.

## Responsibilities

- **Logical Verification**: Checks if the collected data makes sense (e.g., "Does this Quest really belong to this Map?").
- **Approval**: If the data is complete and accurate, they mark the Task as `completed` and send it to the Librarian.
- **Blocking/Rejection**: If the research is insufficient, they block the information and send a "Refinement Directive" back to the Courier for a new round of research.

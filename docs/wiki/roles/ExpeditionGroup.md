![Expedition Group](../images/expedition.svg)

The Expedition Group provides the "Lore Context" that exists outside the raw database files.

## Responsibilities
*   **Online Research**: Accesses Wago.tools, Wowhead, and other community resources.
*   **Lore Bridging**: Explains *why* a specific database entry is important (e.g., "This NPC only spawns during the Lunar Festival").
*   **Version Tracking**: Checks external changelogs to verify when a specific table was added or modified in the live game.
